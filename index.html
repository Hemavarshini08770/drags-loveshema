The operating method

From idea to operating business.

A disciplined path from identifying a real need to building, launching and improving a business.

PHASE 01

Discover

Find a meaningful problem and define who experiences it.

PHASE 02

Validate

Speak with customers and test assumptions with evidence.

PHASE 03

Build

Develop a focused product around the strongest learning.

PHASE 04

Launch

Put the solution in front of real customers and learn.

PHASE 05

Operate

Own the work, support customers and improve delivery.

PHASE 06

Scale

Grow when evidence and operations support the next step.
